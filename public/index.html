How it works

  1. 01

    Track

    MediaPipe follows your hands and face at 30+ fps, entirely in the browser.

  2. 02

    Recognise

    Your trained signs build a sentence; your face adds emotion and grammar.

  3. 03

    Interpret

    An LLM turns ASL order into fluent English, Hindi or Hinglish.

  4. 04

    Speak

    A voice says it with the feeling on your face.
